10 Advanced Indicators Explained Simply

What Each Indicator Tells You

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

What it does: Shows if market is overbought or oversold
How to use: Below 30 = BUY, Above 70 = SELL

MACD

What it does: Shows momentum direction
How to use: Positive = Bullish, Negative = Bearish
Example: "MACD: +19.5" → Bullish momentum

Bollinger Bands

What it does: Shows volatility and potential reversals
How to use: Price touches lower band = possible bounce UP
Price touches upper band = possible bounce DOWN

ATR (Average True Range)

How to use: Higher ATR = more volatility, place stop loss wider
